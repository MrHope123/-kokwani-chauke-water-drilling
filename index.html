About Us

Limpopo's Premier Water Drilling Company

Kokwani Chauke Water Drilling has built a strong reputation across Giyani and Limpopo province for delivering reliable, professional borehole drilling services. With a 363K-strong community of satisfied clients and followers, we are the go-to name when water access matters most.

Based at Baloyi Complex, Office No. 26, R81 Main Road, Giyani — right next to Top Tiles and Nando's — we serve residential, agricultural, and commercial clients with the same commitment to quality every single time.

What We Offer

Complete Borehole Solutions

From drilling to full installation, every package is built to give you clean, reliable water access.

Borehole Drilling

Professional drilling from 40m to 100m depth using modern, well-maintained rigs for maximum accuracy and efficiency.

PVC & Pump Installation

Full pump and PVC pipe installation included in every package, ensuring water flows the moment we complete the job.

Control Box Setup

Electrical control box installation to manage your pump system safely and efficiently — included in full packages.

Steel Casing

1m steel casing at R1,500 and extra steel casing at R1,000 per metre — protecting your borehole for the long term.

Black Pipe Supply

High-quality black pipe supplied and fitted as part of your complete borehole installation package.

Maintenance & Support

Post-installation support and maintenance services to keep your water supply running year-round without interruption.
